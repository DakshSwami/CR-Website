OUR JOURNEY

A Legacy of Engineering

Camber Racing is the official Formula Student combustion team representing SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai. Since our inception over a decade ago, we have been a cornerstone of engineering innovation within the university, consistently pushing the boundaries of automotive design and performance.

The Vision: "Building People, Not Just Cars"

This motto isn't just a slogan; it is our operational philosophy. While we strive to build the fastest, most efficient combustion vehicles, our primary objective is to cultivate the professional and technical growth of our members. We bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world application, ensuring our engineers are industry-ready.

Our team is a multidisciplinary collective of students from various engineering streams. From the initial conceptualization and CAD modeling to the final manufacturing and track testing, every aspect of our vehicle is handled in-house. This hands-on experience in procurement, management, and technical design has led us to build 9 high-performance cars and secure 26 prestigious awards across various national and international competitions.

The Formula Student Challenge

Formula Student is the world's most established educational engineering competition. It challenges student engineers to design, build, and race a single-seater formula-style racing car. The competition is not won by speed alone; it is a holistic evaluation of a team's technical knowledge, financial planning, and dynamic performance.
